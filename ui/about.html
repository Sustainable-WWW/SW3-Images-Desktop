Smart image compression for a lighter, faster and more sustainable web.

What it is

SW3 Images makes photos and graphics dramatically smaller with no visible loss in quality, and can resize them and convert them to WEBP at the same time. Instead of a fixed quality setting, it measures how every image is perceived by the human eye and picks the smallest file that still looks identical.

Who it's for

Anyone who publishes images online — web designers, developers, marketers, shop owners and content creators who want fast-loading websites without spending time tweaking export settings.

Why it matters

Images are the heaviest part of most web pages. Smaller images load faster, rank better in search and use less energy every single time someone views them. Everything happens on your own computer — your images are never uploaded anywhere.
